Treat Trail:KS2 practice with Biscuit

Ten-question lessons. One treat for every correct answer.

Pick your school year, answer ten short questions in maths, English or reading comprehension, and earn a treat for Biscuit with every correct answer. Finish all three and the wardrobe opens until midnight.

Overview

Everything you use daily,
on the phone.

A small routine: ten questions, a treat for each correct answer, and a dog who gets dressed up when the day's mission is done.

  • 01

    Lessons

    Ten questions in each lesson, picked from different topics and mixed fresh every time.

  • 02

    Treats

    Exactly one treat for each correct answer. Nothing is lost if you stop early.

  • 03

    Daily mission

    One maths, one English and one comprehension lesson unlock dress-up for the day.

  • 04

    Biscuit's wardrobe

    24 looks to collect, bought with treats and saved automatically.

  • 05

    Made for your year

    Years 3–6, lower and upper KS2, with a gentle, just-right or stretch challenge.

  • 06

    Works offline

    No account, no internet, no ads. Everything is saved on the device.

How it works

Ten questions, one treat each.

  1. 01

    Ten mixed questions

    Maths, English or reading comprehension, picked from different topics and mixed fresh each time.

  2. 02

    Instant feedback

    Tap an answer and see straight away whether it is right, plus a short explanation.

  3. 03

    One treat per correct answer

    Exactly one. No bonuses, no multipliers. Finish all three subjects to open the wardrobe.

Screens

Home, lessons, and the wardrobe.

Privacy

What stays on your phone.

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Lessons, treats, outfits and settings are stored only on the device.

The app does not need an internet connection. If you uninstall it, that progress is gone.

  • No crash reporting
