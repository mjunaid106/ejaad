Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

ejaad is a small software consultancy. This page explains what happens on this website. It does not replace the privacy notes for the apps themselves.

Analytics on this website

This website uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used: pages visited, clicks, scrolling, device and browser information, an approximate region derived from the connection, and session recordings.

Clarity loads only if you choose “Accept” in the consent banner. If you decline, no Clarity script or cookie is set. When accepted, Clarity may set cookies and local storage (for example _clck and _clsk) in your browser. The contact form is marked to be masked, so the name, email and message are not captured in recordings.

You can change your choice at any time with Manage analytics in the footer of the homepage, or by clearing site data for this domain. Microsoft’s handling of Clarity data is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What this website does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in on this website.
  • No advertising, no selling of personal data.
  • The contact form does not submit to a server. It composes a message in your email client.

The apps

Adhan: Prayer Times, Cat Island and Treat Trail Learning are separate Android apps. Their product pages describe their own data practices. Their progress and settings stay on the device, and the website analytics above do not apply to app usage. The “no analytics” statements on the app pages describe the apps themselves, not this marketing site.
