04 — About

Named for creation.
Built for delivery.

Ijād means creation — bringing something into existence that wasn't there before. That is the whole job.

eJaad is a small consultancy of senior engineers who take an idea, argue with it honestly, and then build the version that survives contact with real users.

We work across four practices — AI, mobile apps and games, web, and corporate systems — because modern products rarely stay in one lane. A mobile game needs a backend and a store page. An ERP needs a mobile companion. A website needs an assistant. One team means one architecture and one place to ask questions.

  • Direct access. You talk to the people writing the code.

  • Nothing held hostage. Repos, infrastructure, and documentation are yours.
