Adhan · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Last updated 19 November 2026.

App: Adhan: Prayer Times, package uk.co.ejaad.adhan (launcher label “Adhan”)
Developer: ejaad
Contact: hello@ejaad.co.uk

Adhan is a prayer-time app for Android. It calculates the five daily prayer times for your location, plays the adhan, shows the Qibla direction, and lists mosques from a dataset that is built into the app.

This policy describes what the app does with information on your phone. It covers the app only — not the Google Play Store, your phone's operating system, a speaker you choose to cast to, or a map or browser app you choose to open.

What the app does

  • Calculates the five daily prayer times on your device from your coordinates and your calculation settings.
  • Plays the adhan at prayer time, from recordings included in the app or imported by you, with an optional prepare reminder before the adhan.
  • Shows the Qibla direction for your location.
  • Lists nearby mosques from a dataset bundled with the app, ranked by distance on the device. The data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the ODbL. Each region carries its own date, read from the data itself: Great Britain as of 12 November 2026 and Pakistan as of 17 November 2026. The United States is not included; where the app has no list for your area it says the list is unavailable rather than showing an empty or invented one.
  • Can cast the adhan audio to a Google Cast speaker on your own Wi-Fi, when you ask it to.

There is no account and no sign-in. Every feature works without one.

Information used on your device

Location (coarse). If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location. It uses the coordinates on the device to calculate prayer times, to show the Qibla direction, and to rank the bundled mosque list by distance. The coordinates are saved in the app's private settings so it does not need to ask again. They are not transmitted anywhere by the app, which does not request background location and does not read your location while it is closed. If you decline, you can enter coordinates manually and use the same features.

Settings. Your calculation settings, saved location, muted prayers and playback choices are stored in the app's private storage (Android DataStore). Other apps cannot read them.

Diagnostics. The app keeps a local log of its own events so a problem can be diagnosed from the device: a rotating file capped at 4 MB, plus a list of the 500 most recent events. The oldest entries are overwritten as new ones arrive. Nothing here is uploaded. The Diagnostics screen can also write a report for you to share; the app hands it to Android's share sheet and sends nothing itself, so it leaves the phone only if you choose where it goes. In that report, coordinates are rounded to about a kilometre and street addresses are left out; speaker addresses stay, because they only mean anything on your own Wi-Fi.

Imported audio. If you import an adhan recording, the file is copied into the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it.

No cloud backup. Android backup is disabled (allowBackup=false), so none of the above is copied to Google's backup service.

What is sent off your device

Nothing is sent to us. There is no account, no ads, no analytics or telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising identifier. The app does not include Google Play Services, Firebase or Google Mobile Services, and there is no server that we operate.

The app declares the INTERNET permission. The app's only network use is casting, described next.

Casting

Casting happens only when you choose a speaker in the app, and only while your phone and the speaker are on the same Wi-Fi network. To cast, the phone:

  • finds the speaker on the local network using mDNS/NSD discovery;
  • opens a TLS connection directly to the speaker;
  • runs a short-lived HTTP server on the phone that serves the audio file to that speaker, and stops it when playback ends.

That traffic stays on your local network, between your phone and the speaker you chose. It does not go over the internet and it does not pass through a server we run. The speaker is a third-party device, so its maker's privacy practices apply to it. If you never cast, the app makes no network connections of its own.

Links you choose to open

  • Open in maps hands a mosque's coordinates to the map app you have chosen on your phone, and that app opens. We do not choose the app and we receive nothing. The map app's privacy policy applies from that point.
  • View on OpenStreetMap opens the OpenStreetMap page for a mosque in your browser, or whichever app handles the link. That app then contacts OpenStreetMap.
  • The app also shows credit lines for its data and audio, including web addresses for OpenStreetMap, Wikimedia Commons and freesound. These are displayed as text; the app does not load them. If you open one yourself, that site's privacy policy applies.

The full credits and licence notes — including the OpenStreetMap/ODbL terms for the bundled mosque data and the licence for each bundled recording — are on the app's Licences screen, and on the credits section of the Adhan page.

The app has no built-in browser and no WebView.

Retention

Everything the app stores stays on your phone until you change it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

  • The diagnostics log is capped at 4 MB across four files, and the recent-events list at 500 entries; older entries are overwritten.
  • Clearing storage or uninstalling removes your settings, saved coordinates, diagnostics and imported audio.
  • We hold no copy of any of it, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.

Children

Adhan is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no data collection from anyone, including children. A child who uses the app gets the same on-device behaviour described here. If you have a concern about a child's use of the app, contact us using the details under Contact, below.

Your choices

  • Location: decline the permission and enter coordinates manually, or turn the permission off later in Android settings. Prayer times, the adhan, the Qibla and the mosque list still work.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: turn them off in Android settings. The app will not sound the adhan.
  • Sound: mute individual prayers, adjust the volume, or stop an adhan while it is playing.
  • Casting: leave the speaker unselected. No casting means no local-network traffic from the app.
  • Data: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. This removes everything the app has stored.
  • Network: the core features work with no internet connection at all.
