Cat
Island

A tropical side-scrolling cat obstacle course.

Pick a cat, run and jump across island platforms, dodge coral spikes, collect fish coins, and race to the goal flag.

  • Android
  • Plays offline
  • Local progress
  • No ads

Overview

Pick a cat. Reach the flag.

Two courses, three zones each, and a shop that keeps the next run interesting.

  • 01

    Two courses

    Course 1 has 32 islands. Course 2 has 38, with a stormier, more demanding layout.

  • 02

    Three zones

    Every course ramps from Easy to Medium to Hard, with the zone name and colour on screen.

  • 03

    Two yarn hearts

    Coral spikes and falls cost a heart. You respawn behind the hazard; run out and you restart at the last checkpoint.

  • 04

    Fish coins

    Every fish collected is 10 bonus coins on completion. Coins buy boosts in the Lobby Shop.

  • 05

    Five cats

    Ginger, Snow, Midnight, Stripy and the Golden Cat, unlocked by finishing Course 2.

  • 06

    Touch controls

    On-screen left, right and jump with multi-touch, so you can hold a direction and jump at the same time.

Screens

The island, the cats, the run.

Lobby, Cat Select, the Shop, and both courses.

How to play

Three things to watch.

  1. 01

    Move and jump

    Arrow keys on desktop, on-screen controls on mobile. Hold jump in the air with a Flying Boost to fly.

  2. 02

    Watch your hearts

    Two hearts per run. Checkpoints sit around 34% and 70% of the course.

  3. 03

    Reach the flag

    Cross the finish for coins and a reward breakdown. Course 1 pays 500 coins plus fish.

Courses

Two courses, one island chain.

Course 01 32 islands

Sunny Shallows to Deep Reef

A clear-sky run through three zones, with generated gaps, stepping stones, bouncy pads and fish arcs.

  • Sunny Shallows · Coral Strait · Deep Reef
  • 500 coins on completion
  • 10 extra coins per fish
Course 02 Golden Cat

Rainy Shallows to Storm Reef

38 islands and a stormier layout. Finish it to unlock the Golden Cat for good.

  • Rainy Shallows · Coral Strait · Storm Reef
  • 1,000 coins on completion, plus fish
  • Unlocked for 20 hours after clearing Course 1
  • Clear Course 1 again to reopen the window

Shop

Three boosts. Twenty seconds each.

Buying a boost while it is active extends the timer.

Speed Boost

20 seconds · 2,000 coins

Run about 45% faster across the islands.

Jump Boost

20 seconds · 2,000 coins

Jump about 30% higher and clear coral spikes more easily; bouncy pads launch higher.

Flying Boost

20 seconds · 10,000 coins

Hold jump while airborne to fly over the course.

The cats

  • Ginger Tabby
  • Snow Cat
  • Midnight Cat
  • Stripy Cat
  • Golden Cat Course 2

All cats play the same. The Golden Cat is cosmetic and unlocked by finishing Course 2.
